# Image licence log (fill this in before launch)

Copyright claims are common and expensive. For every image on the site, keep proof of where it came from and what the licence allows.
The file names suggest some of these are stock photos (for example from Freepik). Free stock licences usually REQUIRE a credit line,
and some do not allow use on a business site without a paid plan. Check each one, then either keep your receipt/licence page or
add the required credit in `IMAGE_CREDIT` in `lib/config.ts` (it shows in the footer).
